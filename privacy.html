Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short Hillgrip collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party code. Your progress stays on your device.

This policy covers the iPhone game Hillgrip: Dirt Bike Trials and this website. Hillgrip is made by Vlad Shchuka, an individual developer. In this policy, “I” means Vlad Shchuka.

1. Data I collect

None. Hillgrip does not collect, send, store or share any personal data. The game has no backend, so there is no server for your data to go to.

Your settings, stars, best times, ghosts, points and Garage setups (tuning, paint, custom bikes and riders) are saved on your device only, in the app’s local storage and files. I cannot see them, and they are removed when you delete the app.

The only information I ever receive is what you choose to send me, for example an email to the support address. I use it to answer you, and I do not share it with anyone.

2. Game Center

Game Center is optional. Hillgrip does not need it, and you can play the whole game without signing in.

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends three things to Game Center leaderboards: your best ranked time on each track and on the Daily Track, the number of tracks where you are first, and your total stars. The game also reads those leaderboards to show the top 100 of a track, your own place and the list of champions.

The scores, and your player identity (such as your player name and avatar), are handled by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. The names and scores of other players come from Apple too. Hillgrip shows only the display name that Game Center provides, and the game has no place to type a name or any other text. Nothing is sent to me or to a server of mine, and I do not receive your name, email or Apple Account details.

You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device’s Settings.

3. Purchases

Hillgrip Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. It is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Your payment details, name and email go to Apple and never reach me.

Hillgrip checks on your device whether Pro has been bought, so it can unlock the content. I see only overall sales figures in App Store Connect, not who bought.

4. Analytics, ads and tracking

None. Hillgrip has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs. It does not read your advertising identifier, so it never shows a tracking permission prompt.

5. This website

These pages are plain HTML and CSS. They use no cookies, no analytics and no scripts, and they load nothing from other sites. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when a page is requested. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that.

6. Children

Hillgrip is not a Kids Category app and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Because the game collects nothing, I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and think your child has sent me personal information, email me and I will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page. Because the game collects no data, I do not expect changes. If a future version of Hillgrip ever collects anything, this policy will say so before that version is released.
