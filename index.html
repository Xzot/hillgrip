Questions and answers

How do the controls work?

The screen has four thumb zones: throttle, brake, lean back and lean forward. Hold throttle to drive, hold brake to slow down or stop, and use the two lean zones to tip the bike backward or forward on slopes and in the air.

Settings has a left-handed option and a tilt option, so you can set the controls up the way your hands like. Game controllers work too, see the controller question below. Short, small corrections usually work better than holding a lean zone down.

Why did I crash?

You crash when the rider’s head hits the ground. After a crash you respawn at the most recent checkpoint, so you do not have to start the whole track again.

If you keep landing on your head, try easing off the throttle in the air and use the lean zones to level the bike before the wheels touch down.

How do stars and unlocks work?

Every track gives up to 3 stars for your finishing time, and a faster run earns more of them. Stars are what unlock the bikes: Scout, Ridge, Hopper, Bruiser and Classic. Collect more stars and more bikes open up.

There is no way to buy stars or skip an unlock. Hillgrip has no in-game currency.

What does Hillgrip Pro include?

Hillgrip Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, $4.99 in the US. In other countries the price follows Apple’s local pricing and is shown before you buy.

  • Worlds 2 to 4, which adds 45 tracks
  • Bikes 3 to 5, which are still earned with stars
  • The Retro LCD look

The free game already has world 1 (15 tracks), the Daily Track and 2 bikes. There is no subscription, so nothing renews.

How do I restore a purchase? Does Family Sharing work?

Open Settings in Hillgrip and tap Restore Purchases. The same button is on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account that bought Pro, and make sure you are online.

Hillgrip Pro supports Family Sharing. If the person who bought it has sharing turned on for purchases, family members can get Pro on their own devices with Restore Purchases.

If Pro still does not show up, check which Apple Account you are signed in with, then email me.

How does the Daily Track work? What are ranked runs?

There is one Daily Track per day and it is the same for everyone. It changes at 08:00 UTC. It is part of the free game.

You can practice it as often as you like. You get 3 ranked runs on each Daily Track, and your best ranked time goes to a Game Center leaderboard.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Hillgrip works without it. Game Center is only used for the Daily Track leaderboard, and only if you are signed in. It is an Apple service, so your player name and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple, as the privacy policy explains.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Hillgrip has no account and no server, so your stars, best times, ghosts and settings live on the phone. If you delete the app, that progress is deleted too, and I cannot get it back because I never receive it.

Purchases are different. They belong to your Apple Account and come back with Restore Purchases.

Can I play with a game controller?

Yes. MFi, Xbox and DualSense controllers work:

  • Right trigger: throttle
  • Left trigger: brake
  • Left stick: lean

Pair the controller in your iPhone’s Bluetooth settings before you start the game.
